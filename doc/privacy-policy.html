XO Rivals – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Developer: Bharat

In short: XO Rivals does not collect, store or share any personal information. The game works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and does not connect to the internet.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the XO Rivals mobile game for Android ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. It applies only to the XO Rivals app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information.

  • Player names. Before a game you can type player names. These names are only used to show whose turn it is and the score on screen. They are kept in your device's memory while the game is open, are erased when you close the app, and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Game results. Scores are only kept for the current session and are erased when you close the app.
  • No device data. We do not collect your device ID, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any other information from your device.

3. Internet and permissions

The app does not request access to the internet and does not ask for any special permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage). The vibration you feel during the game uses your device's built-in haptic feedback, which does not require a permission or share any data.

4. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not share or sell information to anyone, because we do not have any.

The app is downloaded through Google Play. Google may collect information when you use Google Play, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive any personal information about you from Google.

5. Children's privacy

XO Rivals is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

6. Data storage, security and deletion

Since no personal information is collected or stored, there is nothing for us to keep, protect or delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if online features are added), we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version of the app is released.
